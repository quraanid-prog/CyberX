Recipe for a stronger password:

  • Use a long, unique passphrase.

  • Avoid names, birthdays, and reused passwords.
  • Use a password manager and enable multi-factor authentication when available.

6. What If Something Goes Wrong?

If an account or device may be compromised, stay calm and act from a trusted device.

  1. 1. Stop: Do not click further links, reply, or pay a suspicious demand.
  2. 2. Secure accounts: From a trusted device, change affected passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
  3. 3. Contact the provider: Use the official app or website to report the incident and recover access.
  4. 4. Check the device: Update security software, scan the device, and seek trusted technical help if needed.
  5. 5. Report and preserve evidence: Save relevant messages and report fraud to the appropriate local authority. In India, cybercrime can be reported at cybercrime.gov.in.
